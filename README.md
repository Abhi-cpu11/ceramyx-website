# Ceramyx | Website & Digital Marketing

**A real business project where I got to put web development and marketing into practice.**

**Role:** Founder | Website Development & Digital Marketing

**Founded:** August 2026

**Live website:** [ceramyxwpg.com](https://ceramyxwpg.com/)

I founded Ceramyx, an auto detailing business based in South Winnipeg, and built its website and online presence.

I wanted the website to make it easy for customers to understand our services, see examples of completed work, and request an assessment for their vehicle.

## What I worked on

- Helped build and improve the website's layout, content, and overall experience.
- Worked on a five-step assessment form where customers can enter their vehicle details and request a quote.
- Worked on connecting the customer assessment form with supporting online services.
- Worked with Cloudflare to connect our custom domain.
- Created and managed Facebook and Instagram marketing to help bring attention to the business.
- Helped generate interest from approximately 60 potential customers through early marketing efforts.

## Website features

- Information about ceramic coatings, paint correction, and exterior detailing.
- Galleries, videos, and before-and-after comparisons showing completed work.
- A vehicle assessment form with optional photo uploads.
- Form validation and spam protection.
- Mobile-friendly layouts, frequently asked questions, and service information.

## What I learned

Before Ceramyx, most of my development experience came from school projects. This gave me a chance to work on something that people actually use.

I learned more about improving a website based on what a business needs, connecting different online services, and thinking about the customer's experience. I also got to see how website development and marketing work together.

I used ChatGPT as part of the development process, and ChatGPT Sites is where the website is hosted. My involvement included planning the experience, making design and content decisions, working through integrations, and testing changes.
